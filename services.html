Classes and Pricing

Class Types

Gentle Flow

Slow, supported movement that builds flexibility and balance. Ideal for first-time students and anyone returning to exercise.

Vinyasa

A steady, breath-linked flow that builds strength and focus. Options are offered for every pose, so all levels are welcome.

Restorative

Restful poses held with blankets and bolsters to release tension and calm the mind. A great way to end a busy week.

Chair Yoga

Seated and standing poses using a chair for support. Designed for older adults and anyone who prefers to stay off the floor.

Lunchtime Express Flow

A 30-minute class built for busy professionals who want to reset during the workday.

Try a Guided Breathing Practice

Get a feel for our classes with this short guided breathing exercise.

Private Sessions

Private sessions offer one-on-one guidance tailored to your goals, schedule, and comfort level. They are a great choice if you are brand new to yoga, recovering from an injury, or want focused help with alignment.

  • 60-minute sessions at the studio or online
  • Personalized practice plan to use at home
  • Flexible morning, evening, and weekend times
