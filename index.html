Welcome to Riverbend Yoga Studio

Our Mission

At Riverbend Yoga Studio, our mission is to make yoga calm, welcoming, and accessible for every body. We help beginners, busy professionals, and older adults build strength, flexibility, and peace of mind at their own pace.

New to Yoga? You Belong Here

You do not need to be flexible, fit, or experienced to start. Every class offers simpler options, chairs and props are always available, and our instructors will gladly guide you through each pose.

  • No experience needed for Gentle and Beginner classes
  • Mats, blocks, straps, and chairs provided at no charge
  • Small class sizes so you get personal attention

Featured Class: Gentle Flow

Our Gentle Flow class is the perfect place to begin. Over 60 relaxed minutes, you will move through slow, supported poses, learn simple breathing techniques, and finish with a guided relaxation.
