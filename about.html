About Riverbend Yoga Studio

Meet Our Instructors

Maya Torres, Lead Instructor

Maya has taught yoga for over 10 years and holds a 500-hour yoga teacher certification. She specializes in Gentle Flow and Restorative classes and loves helping nervous beginners feel at home on the mat.

Daniel Brooks, Vinyasa and Express Flow Instructor

Daniel is a former office worker who discovered yoga as a way to manage stress. He teaches energizing, approachable Vinyasa and Lunchtime Express classes for busy professionals.

Linda Carter, Chair Yoga Instructor

Linda is certified in adaptive and senior yoga. Her Chair Yoga classes focus on balance, mobility, and confidence for older adults.

Accessibility Accommodations

  • Step-free entrance, accessible restroom, and reserved parking near the door
  • Chairs, blocks, straps, and bolsters available in every class
  • Instructors offer modified options for each pose
  • Large-print schedules and verbal cues for students with low vision
  • Please tell us about any needs before class so we can prepare

Studio Policies

Arrival
Please arrive 10 minutes early for your first class to check in and get set up.
Cancellations
Cancel at least 12 hours before class to keep your class credit.
Late Arrival
Doors close 5 minutes after class begins to protect a calm space for everyone.
Phones
Please silence phones before entering the studio room.
Health and Safety
Let your instructor know about injuries or health concerns, and always move within your comfort level.
